Key findings

No clear boundary rationale in the disclosed record

The records show the final proposed service area, but do not clearly show how the boundary was developed or why certain areas were included.

No documented Canada Post boundary map

Records reference attempts to obtain Canada Post service boundary information, but do not show a formal Canada Post map or confirmation used to draw the final service area.

Options were referenced but not fully documented

Staff materials refer to alternative options being explored, but the disclosed records do not include a structured comparison of those options.

The boundary appears to have been manually developed

One released email refers to the Electoral Area Director marking out a “preferred service area boundary” on a printed map.

Context and limitations

These documents are provided as primary source material so residents, reporters, and taxpayers can review the record directly. The purpose of this page is to make the public record easier to access and understand.

Some records may be redacted to remove personal information unrelated to the public decision-making process. Interpretations may vary. Readers are encouraged to review the source documents themselves.
